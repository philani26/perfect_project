Company background

Trade knowledge, not a startup guess

Perfect L Projects was built to give clients air conditioning and refrigeration solutions they can rely on — quality workmanship, straight communication and products that hold up.

Lungelo Maphumulo leads the company with formal qualifications in air conditioning and refrigeration, and hands-on industry experience behind them. He has also worked as a facilitator at Coastal College, training the next generation of technicians in the trade.

That mix of practical fieldwork, technical qualification and teaching experience is what shapes how Perfect L Projects runs every job.
